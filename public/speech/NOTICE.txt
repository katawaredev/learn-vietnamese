Local changes: the dictionary is loaded from memory instead of a memory-mapped
file; batch scheduling is sequential; a small allocation/initialization ABI is
added. The pronunciation and normalization rules are unchanged.
Rebuild: python scripts/speech/build-phonemizer.py

The application downloads Kokoro Vietnamese, VieNeu v3-Turbo and MOSS codec
weights and voice presets from their pinned upstream Hugging Face repositories.
Their model cards license those artifacts under Apache-2.0. Model weights are
not bundled in this directory. Sources and revisions: docs/ai-models.md.
